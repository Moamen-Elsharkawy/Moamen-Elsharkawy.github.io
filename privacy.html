What this site collects.

Almost nothing. This is a static site: plain HTML, CSS and a small script, served from GitHub Pages. There is no account, no login, no database and no advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, no tracking pixel and no third-party script. Nothing you do here is profiled or sold. Typefaces are served from this site itself rather than from a font CDN, so loading a page does not tell anybody else that you visited.

GitHub, which hosts the files, keeps its own server logs. Those are outside my control and governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

The contact form

The "book a call" button opens a Google Form. Anything you type into it goes to Google and then to me, under Google's privacy policy, not this site's. I use it only to reply to you and to prepare for the call. I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not pass it on to anyone.

If you would rather not use it, email contact@moamen-ai.com instead. Same result, one fewer third party.

Email

Mail sent to that address is handled by my mail provider and kept for as long as it is useful for the conversation we are having. Ask me to delete it and I will.

Your data if we work together

Systems I build for a client run in the client's own accounts under the client's own keys. Their data stays with them. That is a design rule, not a preference, and it is written into how the builds are set up.
